## 💰 Business Model

### Revenue Streams
1. **Pay-per-Opinion**: $25-50 per second opinion
2. **Subscription Plans**: 
   - Basic: $19/month (2 opinions)
   - Premium: $49/month (unlimited)
3. **B2B Partnerships**: 
   - Insurance companies: $5-10/member/month
   - Corporate wellness: $2-5/employee/month

### Pricing Strategy
- **Penetration Pricing**: First opinion free
- **Value-Based Pricing**: 80% cheaper than traditional
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Premium for urgent cases

### Cost Structure
- **Variable**: Doctor commissions (40% of revenue)
- **Fixed**: Platform development, marketing, compliance
- **Operating**: Customer support, server costs
